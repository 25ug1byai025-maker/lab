Binomial PMF Calculator

Calculate the probability of obtaining exactly k successes in n independent trials with a constant probability of success.

Input Parameters

Define the distribution and the number of successes.

Must be a positive integer.

Allowed range: 0 to 1.

Must be an integer between 0 and n.

Probability Result

PMF
MATHEMATICAL EXPRESSION
P(X = k) = C(n,k) pk (1 − p)n−k
Probability P(X = 7)
0.1844
18.44%

What does this mean?

There is approximately an 18.44% chance of getting exactly 7 successes in 20 independent trials when each trial has a 0.35 probability of success.
